Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

CaliGym LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it in compliance with GDPR and CCPA.

2. Data Minimization & Collection

In accordance with our data minimization principle, we collect ONLY the information necessary to provide our calisthenics coaching service:

  • Email address (for account identification and coaching updates)
  • Name (for profile personalization)
  • Fitness level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
  • Age (strictly to verify minimum age requirement of 18+)
  • Workout logs (sets, reps, duration, and notes you input)

3. Third-Party SDKs and Services

We integrate with trusted third-party services for payments and analytics:

  • Stripe: Secure payment processing. We never store credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Vercel Analytics: Anonymous page view and performance metrics.
  • Supabase: Secure database hosting with encryption at rest.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your personal data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You have the right to request full data deletion at any time under GDPR Article 17 and CCPA. To request deletion, submit a request via your Settings page or email us directly at support@caligym.io. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
