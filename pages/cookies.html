Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences and understand how you use CaliGym.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Functional Cookies (Required): Essential for session management, authentication, and cookie consent preferences. Retained for 1 year.
  • Analytics Cookies (Optional): Used via Vercel Analytics to measure site traffic and performance anonymously. Retained for 30 days.
  • Marketing Cookies (Optional): Used to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns. Retained for 90 days.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time through our cookie banner or your browser settings. Declining optional cookies will not affect core site functionality.
